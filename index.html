Questions

What hospitals and centres usually ask first.

If your question isn't here, it's a two-minute WhatsApp away from an answer.

How fast will I get my reports?
Turnaround depends on modality and case complexity, but our day and night desks are built around fast reporting as the default, not the exception. Ask your account contact for typical times on your specific modality mix.
What happens with emergency or critical cases?
Critical findings are phoned to your team immediately, not just emailed or uploaded. Emergency and night cases go to a dedicated desk rather than a general queue.
Do we need new hardware or software?
No. Send studies via PACS, a secure cloud upload link, or WhatsApp for X-rays — whatever you already use day to day.
Who actually reads our studies?
Registered consultant radiologists, matched by modality and case type. Every report goes through a second-read quality check before it's signed off — not just typed and sent.
How is patient data handled?
Studies are shared only through secure channels, accessed only by the radiologist assigned to that case, and used solely to generate your report.
We already have a radiologist, or another reporting partner — is this still useful?
Most of our hospital partners use MTS as backup — nights, emergencies, leave coverage, or overflow during busy periods — rather than a full replacement. There's no long-term lock-in required to try us out on a few cases first.
How does pricing work?
Pricing is based on modality and volume, with no hidden charges. Ask us for a rate card specific to your case mix.
Which cities do you cover?
We're strongest across Maharashtra and growing pan-India. If your city isn't covered yet, ask — it's often a quick fix rather than a hard no.
